## Manual Sourcing Framework (Free)

To reach the final 100 *qualified* prospects today without paid tools, use these advanced Google Search Operators (Google Dorks) to legally scrape the web for free.

### 1. The "Recent Funding" Hack
Companies that just raised money have millions in the bank and need to upgrade their branding. Search this exactly in Google:
`"smart home" OR "home automation" "Series A" OR "Series B" OR "seed round" "announced" 2025 OR 2026 site:techcrunch.com`

### 2. The "Y Combinator" Hack
Y Combinator startups are heavily funded and easy to reach. Search:
`"smart home" OR "IoT" site:ycombinator.com/companies`

### 3. Finding the Email Format
Once you find a company (e.g., "SmartCompany"), go to Google and search:
`*@smartcompany.com` OR `"email * * smartcompany.com"`
Look at the bolded results in the descriptions. If you see one employee is `j.smith@smartcompany.com`, then the CEO's email is likely `firstinitial.lastname@smartcompany.com`.

### 4. LinkedIn Bypass Hack
To find founders without a LinkedIn Premium account, use X-Ray searching. Paste this in Google:
`site:linkedin.com/in/ "Founder" OR "CEO" "Smart Home" OR "Home Automation" -intitle:"profiles"`
