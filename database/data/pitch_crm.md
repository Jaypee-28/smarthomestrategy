# SmartHomeStrategy Pitch CRM

**The Wall Street Philosophy for Cold Emailing High-Net-Worth Founders:**
1. **No fluff.** Do not say "I hope this finds you well." 
2. **Short.** If it cannot be read on an iPhone lock screen without scrolling, it's too long.
3. **Pattern Interrupt.** Speak to them like an equal, not a salesperson. You are offering them an exclusive M&A asset, not asking for a favor.
